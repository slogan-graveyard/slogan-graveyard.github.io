The Paper Shield

How trusts and loopholes turn wealth into a ghost.

The most sophisticated tool in the arsenal of the wealthy is not an investment portfolio or a business venture. It is the trust fund. By legally separating oneself from their assets, the wealthy can enjoy the fruits of their wealth while maintaining the legal status of a pauper.

When a person claims they are "too poor" to pay for their care because their assets are held in a trust, they are utilizing a paper shield. The trust is not a financial vehicle; it is a bureaucratic mask. It allows the individual to say "I don't own this," while continuing to benefit from its existence. It is the ultimate dodge: using the law to escape the social responsibility that comes with wealth.

This creates a moral hazard in our welfare systems. We have allowed the definition of "need" to be manipulated by lawyers. If you are wealthy enough to employ a lawyer to hide your assets, you are not in financial need of state support.

We must stop accepting "legal ownership" as the sole metric for means-testing. We need a system that recognizes beneficial interest. If you can enjoy the wealth, you should be responsible for the costs of your life.

A trust is not a shield from reality; it is a way of pretending that reality doesn't apply to you. It’s time to pull back the curtain on paper poverty.
